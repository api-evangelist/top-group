TOP Group (The Open Platform, top.co) is the tech company fueling the Telegram economy, connecting Telegram's billion-user audience to the TON blockchain through in-house retail products and builder solutions. TOP operates two flagship companies: Wallet in Telegram (100M+ users) and TOP Labs, a venture builder behind 25+ Telegram/TON ecosystem projects including Tonkeeper (acquired 100% in January 2026), TON API, Getgems, STON.fi, Notcoin, and Tribute. Founded by Andrew Rogozov and backed by Ribbit Capital, TOP is the first unicorn in the TON-Telegram ecosystem, valued at $1 billion. Its developer surface centers on TON API (tonapi.io) - the indexed REST, streaming, and webhooks API to the TON blockchain - and the TON Console developer platform (tonconsole.com). Surfaced as a portfolio company of ribbit-capital and enriched by the API Evangelist pipeline.

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for TOP Group (The Open Platform): the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
